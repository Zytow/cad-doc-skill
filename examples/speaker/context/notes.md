# Notes

## General

- **Render style: printed parts light grey on a grey studio background, no orange.** Example of a style decision that lives in the notes, not on the page.

## Tools

- **Never save the NX session.** Display changes are reset, exports change nothing.
- **Dense meshes such as populated boards slow down the line pass of `render_assembly.py`.** The tool decimates them for that pass only.
